From the board

Letter from the Board

A welcome from the Board of Directors of the Hispanic Society of Kamloops.

Dear friends,

Kamloops is falling in love with Latin culture, and we could not be happier. More and more neighbours are asking where to dance, taste, learn Spanish, and celebrate with us.

The Hispanic Society of Kamloops is ready. We are committed to bringing Latin culture to every corner of our city and to making sure everyone feels included, whether you were born in Latin America or are simply curious about its music, flavours, and traditions.

Since 2019 we have shared our dance, food, and heritage through celebrations, family events, and partnerships with local organizations. With our volunteers, dancers, and sponsors, we want to open even more doors.

There is a place for you here. Come hungry, come curious, and come celebrate with us!

With gratitude,

The Board of Directors, Hispanic Society of Kamloops
