Alma Latina

Give our dancers their wings

Every skirt that spins on stage took hours of sewing, saving, and love. Your gift helps Alma Latina grow: costumes, dance shoes, travel to perform, and workshops that bring new dancers in. Your gift and every booking also help us fund a stipend for each dancer at every event, starting small and growing over time. And because Alma Latina raises funds for the Hispanic Society of Kamloops, every dollar keeps working for the whole community.

About

Our mission

The Hispanic Society of Kamloops preserves and shares Hispanic and Latin American culture through celebrations, dance, language, and community programs, and welcomes newcomers and neighbours of every background to learn, belong, and give back.

Mission, vision, and values are proposed texts, pending approval by the board.
