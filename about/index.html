About

Who we are

Founded in 2019, the Hispanic Society of Kamloops is a bridge between our diverse Hispanic community and the wider Kamloops community, fostering cultural understanding and appreciation through programs and events. It was incorporated on 18 April 2019 as Sociedad Hispano-Canadiense Thompson Okanagan Hispanic-Canadian Society, and adopted its current name on 7 January 2022.

Mission and vision

Our mission

The Hispanic Society of Kamloops preserves and shares Hispanic and Latin American culture through celebrations, dance, language, and community programs, and welcomes newcomers and neighbours of every background to learn, belong, and give back.

Our vision

A Kamloops where Hispanic culture is a visible, loved, and shared part of everyday community life.

The Society's purposes

These five purposes were published on the Society's former website. Whether they match the registered constitution is still to be confirmed.

  1. To offer non-financial, non-immigration assistance, moral support, suggestions, friendship, and regional information to newly arrived Hispanic immigrants and families and to visitors to our region.
  2. To promote and preserve our cultural heritage within the Thompson-Okanagan community of British Columbia.
  3. To maintain and strengthen the use of the Spanish language in the Okanagan, BC.
  4. To promote recreational programs and community events that advance multiculturalism and the well-being of our community.
  5. To provide opportunities for families to socialize, take part, and give back to the communities where they live.

A short timeline

2019: registered as a BC society. 2022: adopted the current name. 2024: Rosca de Reyes, the México de mis Amores gala, Hispanic Heritage Month at the library, and museum collaborations. 2025: Hispanic Tales & Crafts, Mother's Day, and Fiesta Latina at the DoubleTree. 2026: Mother's Day, Father's Day, Cinema Passport with the Kamloops Film Society, Fiesta Latina at O.L.P.H., and Día de los Muertos with the museum. Milestones from 2019 to 2023 that are not in the public record are still to be confirmed.
