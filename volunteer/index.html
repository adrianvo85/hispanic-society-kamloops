Join the family: volunteer with us

Our celebrations run on neighbours who show up with time, talent, and a big smile. Come practice your Spanish, share your skills, make friends, and help bring Hispanic culture to life in Kamloops. No experience needed, just ganas.

Why join

A place at the table

Celebrate with us

Help host cultural gatherings in Kamloops — from family days to evening fiestas.

Meet your neighbours

Volunteer shifts are a chance to practice Spanish or English and make new friends.

Share your skills

Setup, welcome, food, activities, photography, or planning — there is a role for different gifts.

Flexible involvement

Help for one event or throughout the year. Tell us what time you can give.

A letter or volunteer hours

A reference letter or confirmation of volunteer hours may be available — pending board approval of the process.

Some benefits and requirements (reference letters, student hours, background checks, Serving It Right) are still to be confirmed by the board.

Ways to help

Volunteer roles

These are the volunteer roles proposed for the Society. No names are published here.

Setup and welcome

Greet guests, help set up and pack down.

Food and kitchen

Support community meals and food service.

Family activities

Crafts, games, and stories for kids.

Photography

Capture the best moments (with image permissions).

Language support

ES/EN translation and conversation.

Planning support

Help the events committee.

Dance group support

Costumes, music, rehearsals, and performance logistics.

Language-exchange host

Host Spanish/English conversation tables.

Sponsor outreach

Help visit local businesses with the sponsorship package.

Social media and translation

Bilingual posts, posters, and photos.

Newcomer welcome

Accompany newly arrived families (the Society's first published purpose).
