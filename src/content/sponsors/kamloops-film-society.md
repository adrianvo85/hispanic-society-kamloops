The Kamloops Film Society is a local non-profit that runs the Paramount Theatre, bringing film to downtown Kamloops. With the Society it presented a Chilean cinema night with live music and food (20 September 2026) as part of Cinema Passport. The Kamloops Film Society opened the Paramount Theatre to Latin American cinema with us. Thank you!
