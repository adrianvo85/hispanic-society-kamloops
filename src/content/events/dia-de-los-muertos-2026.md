A free, all-ages celebration of Día de los Muertos with the Hispanic Society of Kamloops on site at Kamloops Museum and Archives. The museum advertised music, crafts, and learning about this joyful holiday that helps families honour their ancestors. Registration was directed to Kamloops.ca/Register.
