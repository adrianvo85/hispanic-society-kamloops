Stand with the community

By partnering with the Hispanic Society of Kamloops, your organization connects with our community while showing its commitment to diversity, inclusion, culture, and community development. Our annual celebration has brought Hispanic and non-Hispanic neighbours together for more than six years, and your support helps us keep it going all year. Annual packages last 12 months. Per-event support starts at $150. In-kind gifts are welcome. All figures are in Canadian dollars.

Guidelines

  • Sponsorships run for 12 months from the date of acceptance, unless a per-event package is agreed.
  • Every sponsorship requires board approval.
  • Political parties are not eligible.
  • Approved sponsor logos are added to the website within 10 days of receiving a usable file.
  • Payment is accepted via Zeffy or electronic transfer.
  • A tax receipt is issued only if the Society is confirmed as a CRA-registered charity. Otherwise the Society issues a sponsorship invoice.
  • Sponsors receive a year-end impact report.
